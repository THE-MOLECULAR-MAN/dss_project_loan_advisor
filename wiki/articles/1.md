# Start here

## What this project does

A loan officer's assistant. Loan requests and customer details are cleaned and joined into one view of 2,500 applications; a model scores the pending ones for their probability of default; and the **Loan Advisor - Visual Agent** answers questions about an application, the bank's loan and credit policies and the model's risk score in plain language. The scores support a loan officer's decision and never replace it.

## A 5-minute tour

1. Open the Flow. Zone **Inputs**: two uploaded files (loan requests, customer info) are synchronized into tables.
2. Zone **Data Prep**: ages and FICO scores are derived, loan fields are standardized, and the two sources are joined into [Customers_and_Loan](DATASET:Customers_and_Loan).
3. Zone **Train model to predict loan defaulting**: applications with a known outcome train the model; the pending ones are scored into [Default_unknown_scored](DATASET:Default_unknown_scored).
4. Zone **Agents**: the loan and credit policy documents are embedded into a knowledge bank that the agent searches.
5. Open the [Loan Advisor dashboard](DASHBOARD:D96Ngmk) for the business view, then ask the agent: "What is the status of application 60968, and is it likely to default?"
6. Zone **Extensions** holds what makes the project production-ready: the model evaluation store and, outside the Flow, the checks and the scenario listed below.

## Why Dataiku (illustrative figures)

Without a platform, this work is spread across a spreadsheet of applications, a policy PDF on a shared drive, a scoring script on a laptop and an undocumented model, and the officer opens each one in turn. Here one place covers them, with every step documented and checked.

The following numbers are assumptions (project variables named `value_*`), not measurements:
- Applications per month: **2,000** (variable `value_applications_per_month` = 2000).
- Minutes saved per application by asking one agent instead of searching three sources: **8** (variable `value_minutes_saved_per_application` = 8).
- Months per year: 12 (variable `value_months_per_year`).
- That is 2000 x 8 x 12 / 60 = **about 3,200 officer hours a year** (illustrative figures).

## Production-ready touches

- **Data quality rules** on [Loan_Requests](DATASET:Loan_Requests), [Customer_Info](DATASET:Customer_Info), [Customers_and_Loan_prepared](DATASET:Customers_and_Loan_prepared) and [Default_unknown_scored](DATASET:Default_unknown_scored): keys not empty and unique, ranges for amounts, income, age, FICO and probabilities.
- **Scenario** [Score new loan applications](SCENARIO:Score_new_loan_applications): checks the inputs, rebuilds the scored applications, checks the outputs, with mail notifications; scheduled on weekdays at 07:00 US Eastern and switched off until you turn it on.
- **Semantic model** "Loan applications": business names and measures (applications, default rate, average interest rate, predicted defaults).
- **Agent review**: "Loan advisor review" holds six test questions (status lookup, default risk, policy search, counts, a refusal to approve, a refusal to share personal data); results appear in the GenAI evaluation store "Loan advisor quality" and the comparison "Loan advisor versions".
- **Agent Hub** for business users, **Trace Explorer** for builders.
